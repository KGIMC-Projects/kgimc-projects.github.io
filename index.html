Who We Are

Exploring Faith & Spirituality

Discover the depth of faith and the beauty of spiritual growth.

At KGIMC, we explore the timeless truths and principles found in the Word of God that shape beliefs and transform lives. By understanding and embracing Kingdom truth, believers are equipped, healed, awakened, and sent out to impact the world.

We love God and we love people. Our mission is to see lives transformed as individuals step into their God-given calling and purpose — walking in the fullness of who they were created to be.

We are Guardians of the Earth — called to raise sons and daughters who know how to rule and reign in their God-given dominion and Kingdom identity.

Our Mandate

Four Pillars of Our Purpose

To bring these to all people, races, and nations through the redemptive power of Jesus Christ.

✝

Healing

Restoring the wounded in body, mind, and spirit through the power of Jesus Christ.

✝

Deliverance

Setting captives free from every yoke and bondage of the enemy.

✝

Reconciliation

Bringing people back to God, to family, and to one another in love.

✝

Restoration

Rebuilding lives, families, and nations back to God's original design.
